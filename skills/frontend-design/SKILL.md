# Frontend Design Skill

## Visual & Interaction Principles
1. **Palette:** Restrained modern colors (Zinc/Slate base, single warm accent, emerald success, red error). No bright neon overload.
2. **Typography & Layout:** Clean hierarchies, generous breathing room, rounded borders (`╭ ╮ ╯ ╰`).
3. **Flicker-Free Terminal:** Synchronized output (`CSI 2026`) and differential line rendering.
4. **Honest Feedback:** Immediate visual feedback on background tasks without fake progress animations.
